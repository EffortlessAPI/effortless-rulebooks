ƒ Final Score Calculated 1 hop from ground truth number DR-12
In RuleSpeak®

The score result's final score is determined by the following priority:

  1. 0 if the disqualified flag is set
  2. the largest of 0 and the fit score plus the archetype score plus the history score plus the comp score plus the culture score minus the negative score in all other cases
In plain words

If is disqualified then 0, otherwise the largest of 0 and (((((fit score plus archetype score) plus history score) plus comp score) plus culture score) minus negative score).

As a formula
= IF(Is Disqualified, 0, MAX(0, Fit Score + Archetype Score + History Score + Comp Score + Culture Score - Negative Score))
Inputs 7 fields this rule reads

Based on cosine distance in resume ChromaDB collection. ●Archetype ScoreScore Result Match to role archetypes (0.0 - 1.0). Based on cosine distance in role_archetypes collection. ●History ScoreScore Result Match to past accepted decisions (0.0 - 1.0). Only 'yes' verdicts contribute. ●Comp ScoreScore Result Compensation alignment with base salary (0.0 - 1.0). Non-linear curve; 0.5 when comp is missing. ●Culture ScoreScore Result Match to positive culture/quality dimensions (0.0 - 1.0). ●Negative ScoreScore Result Penalty score from negative signal matches (0.0 - 1.0). Subtracted from positive scores.
