The event's coverage percent is determined by the following priority:

  1. the total slots filled divided by the total slots needed if the total slots needed is greater than 0
  2. 0 in all other cases

ƒ Staffing Grade Calculated 4 hops from ground truth string DR-5
In RuleSpeak®

The event's staffing grade is determined by the following priority:

  1. “A” if the coverage percent is at least 0.95
  2. “B” if the coverage percent is at least 0.85
  3. “C” if the coverage percent is at least 0.70
  4. “D” if the coverage percent is at least 0.50
  5. “F” in all other cases
In plain words

If coverage percent is at least 0.95 then "A", otherwise if coverage percent is at least 0.85 then "B", otherwise if coverage percent is at least 0.70 then "C", otherwise if coverage percent is at least 0.50 then "D", otherwise "F".

As a formula
= IF(Coverage Percent>=0.95, "A", IF(Coverage Percent>=0.85, "B", IF(Coverage Percent>=0.70, "C", IF(Coverage Percent>=0.50, "D", "F"))))

ƒ Load Status Calculated 3 hops from ground truth string DR-9
In RuleSpeak®

The volunteer's load status is determined by the following priority:

  1. “over” if the assigned hours is greater than the max hours, in all other cases “under” if the assigned hours is less than the max hours times 0.5, in all other cases “ok” if the max hours is greater than 0
  2. “under” in all other cases
In plain words

If max hours is greater than 0 then if assigned hours is greater than max hours then "over", otherwise if assigned hours is less than (max hours times 0.5) then "under", otherwise "ok", otherwise "under".

As a formula
= IF(Max Hours>0, IF(Assigned Hours>Max Hours, "over", IF(Assigned Hours<(Max Hours*0.5), "under", "ok")), "under")
Inputs 2 fields this rule reads

In RuleSpeak®

The shift's coverage status is determined by the following priority:

  1. “understaffed” if the filled count is less than the needed count
  2. “overstaffed” if the filled count is greater than the needed count
  3. “covered” in all other cases
In plain words
