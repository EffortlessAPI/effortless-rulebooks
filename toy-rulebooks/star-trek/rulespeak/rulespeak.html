The average fan rating: RatingTotal / RatingCount, and 0 for a series nobody has rated yet. It was =SUMIFS(...) until 2026-11-26, which made it the total, not the average this table and IsGood describe (TOS read 13 where the rulebook says 4.33).

The series's rating is determined by the following priority:

  1. 0 if the rating count is 0
  2. the rating total divided by the rating count in all other cases
In plain words
