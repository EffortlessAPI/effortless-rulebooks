The questions this role asks

Each question is in the role's own voice. The witnessed answer is the substrate's reading of the predicates that were invented to answer it.

Before I execute close-01, close-02, or close-07: is my own role assignment currently valid, and is the agent named in that assignment actually me?

I am a pipeline. I must not act on a step whose role has been reassigned out from under me, or whose assignment window has lapsed. `ra-notify-pipeline-2026` proves assignments can be scoped to a platform release; a v5.3 rollout could supersede close-pipeline's authority mid-quarter and today NOTHING would stop me from posting entries anyway. This is my single most important pre-condition.

For each step I am about to run, has every blocking requirement attached to it actually been evaluated — or am I about to execute past a control that nobody scored?

`sr-c01` binds `req-close-cutoff` (blocking) to close-01 and `sat-close-cutoff` scores it. But `sr-c03b` binds `req-close-evidence` to close-03 and `sr-c08` binds it to close-08 — and there is no RequirementSatisfaction row for either. A blocking retention control on the step where I archive evidence is completely unevaluated. I need to see that as a number, not infer it from an absence.

q-close-auto-blocking-requirements-evaluated

Am I drifting? Are humans overriding or correcting my output more often now than they used to?

This is my self-assessment question and it is the one I currently cannot ask at all. `se-close02` was corrected by a human (controller-approved rerun) and that fact exists only as prose in a Deviation string. If my override rate is climbing across quarters, the model should say so as a number before a human notices as a feeling. Note that a rising override rate is not automatically bad — it may mean humans are watching more closely — but it must be visible either way.

`issue-close-feed` already happened to me — a feed was 17 minutes old against a 15-minute SLA on `bind-close-erp`, and I extracted anyway. `verify-feed-time` states the expectation as a signal, but nothing in the model refuses to let me run. I need per-step aggregate freshness, not a per-binding fact I have to interpret myself.
