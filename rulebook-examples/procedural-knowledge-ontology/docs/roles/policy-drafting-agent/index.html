Before `notification-pipeline` sends anything I rendered at policy-06: has a human actually approved the MEANING at policy-05, and has counsel actually signed off at policy-04 — or is my text about to reach employees on the strength of two pending reviews?

The exec is live right now. `se-policy04` is InProgress, `sat-policy-legal` is PartiallySatisfied ('Review started; final approval pending'), and there is no step execution for policy-05 at all. Both `req-policy-legal` and `req-policy-human-approval` are blocking. The step ordering says policy-06 comes after policy-05 — but ordering is a `StepTransitions` row, not a gate. Nothing in the model prevents policy-06 from executing while the two human approvals ahead of it are unfinished, and I am the agent that would execute it. I want the gate to exist before I am the reason it was needed.

This is my boundary and it is the model's clearest example of a ratified constraint with zero enforcement. `kf-policy-ai-boundary` is Approved, Confidence High, sourced from noah-williams during `elicit-policy-interview`, owned by employment-counsel, IsCurrentlyValid TRUE. Nothing in fifty tables tests it. `req-policy-human-approval` says the same thing from the requirement side and is also blocking — and `sr-p05` binds it to policy-05, a step I do not execute, so even that requirement never looks at my output. I want my own leash to be real.

q-policy-ai-commitment-boundary

Is my draft surviving human review, or is `noah-williams` rewriting my legal language every time?

My self-assessment question. `se-policy04` is the legal review of my policy-03 draft and it is still InProgress with `sat-policy-legal` at PartiallySatisfied. If counsel systematically rewrites what I produce, I am not drafting — I am generating a first pass that costs a lawyer more time than it saves, and the org should know that as a rate rather than as a lawyer's private opinion. Note the correct reading is ambiguous: a high rewrite rate might mean I am bad, or might mean counsel is doing exactly the job the boundary assigns them. The number does not settle that; it makes the conversation possible.

There is not. `RoleAssignments` contains rows for finance-analyst, controller, cfo, hr-policy-owner, employment-counsel, notification-publisher, and both sides of variance-review-agent.
