📘 South Park ontology, in SDLAF Docs

Jessica Talisman's South Park ontology as an ordinary rulebook: her ten stated facts are rows and columns of the tables they are about (people, parenthoods, marriages, friendships, grade and role records), and each of her five rules is a field: the inverse is the parenthood read from the child's end, the symmetric rule a link with no direction counted from both ends, the property chain a lookup through two parenthoods, domain and range a formula and a count on each individual, disjointness a check on each parenthood. Every rule is switched by the one Reasoning row. Nothing is a generic subject-property-object store: Schema, Data, Lookups, Aggregations and Formulas only.

Declarative business rules rendered from the rulebook — the single source of truth. Every statement below is the plain-language reading of a formula; nothing here is hand-authored prose.
